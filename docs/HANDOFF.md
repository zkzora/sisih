sponsored by the operator.

Why only 3.00 USDC of operator float: on mainnet advance-cycle runs **pay due bills -> sweep payees
and merchants back to the operator -> deposit simulated income -> fund reserves**. Cycle 1 returns
17.30 (bills) + 1.70 (merchant receipts) before the 17.00 income is deposited, so the income is
recycled money. Cycles 2 to 5 bill 15.40 to 16.20 plus agent spend, so they run at most about 1 USDC
short each. The testnet order (income first) would need about 15.30 USDC of float.
